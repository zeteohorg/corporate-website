Zeteoh, Inc. will exhibit in the Tokyo Metropolitan Government-Recommended Startup & Venture Zone at "Tokyo International Industry Exhibition 2026," to be held at Tokyo Big Sight from Wednesday, November 11 to Friday, November 13, 2026.

Tokyo International Industry Exhibition is one of Japan's largest comprehensive trade shows, bringing together small and medium-sized enterprises and startups from the Tokyo metropolitan area with outstanding technologies and products. About 600 companies and organizations from Japan and abroad are exhibiting this year.

Zeteoh's spatial AI "TRAILS" achieves indoor positioning with only a wearable device, without any Wi-Fi, beacon or other infrastructure installation. Deployment costs are about one-tenth of conventional indoor positioning. The AI learns the regularities of human movement within a space from the facility's floor plan and corrects position estimates, achieving an error of 1–3 m. In a trial with Central Japan Railway Company (JR Central), TRAILS kept the error within 3 m even inside a Shinkansen bullet train running at up to 285 km/h, and it received the Next Generation Award at the CEATEC AWARD 2026.

### Booth Highlights

- **Live demo:** Walk with a single smartphone and see your path drawn in real time.
- **3D visualization:** The captured flow lines are overlaid on a digital twin, so you can see at a glance where people passed and where they stopped.
- **Spatial AI camera "Astra":** As an example of spatial AI implemented in a wearable, we will exhibit a wearable device under development. It records position and video at the same time, so it keeps a record of "what was seen, and where."

### Who Should Visit

- Those who want to check whether flow lines on site contain waste
- Those who want to know where workers are right now
- Those who want to confirm that staff covered every checkpoint during equipment inspections
- Those who want to understand how veterans and newcomers work differently

We start with small-scale pilot deployments. You are welcome to consult us even if you limit the scope to specific sites or processes, so please stop by our booth. We look forward to seeing you there.
